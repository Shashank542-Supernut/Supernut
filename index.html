WHO WE ARE

India's Dedicated B2B Fox Nut (Makhana) Export House

The Supernut Company operates exclusively in the global B2B trade of raw and processed Fox Nuts (Gorgon Nut / Euryale Ferox). Headquartered in India with direct sourcing operations in Bihar—the world's capital of Fox Nut cultivation—we bridge the gap between traditional harvest expertise and modern international quality compliance.

Bihar Origin Advantage

Over 90% of the world's highest grade Fox Nuts originate in Bihar, India. Our direct farmer contracts ensure unadulterated quality and complete supply chain transparency from farm pond to export container.

Optical Sorter Machine Grading

We deploy modern color sorting and mechanical sizing screens to separate raw popping into precise millimetric Suta grades (Grade 5+ and Grade 6+), eliminating black spots and immature seeds.

Scalable Bulk Supply

Whether you require 20ft Full Container Loads (FCL) or 40ft High Cube containers, our warehousing and packaging infrastructure delivers consistent quality shipment after shipment.
